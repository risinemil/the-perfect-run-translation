- Eugène-Henry von Schrodinger → Эжен-Анри фон Шрёдингер — Len's cat. French first name + German surname. Transliterated with standard Russian rendering of Eugène and Schrödinger.
- Mr. Looking Glass → мистер Зазеркалье — Shroud's alternate alias used once. "Looking Glass" = зеркало/зазеркалье; chose "Зазеркалье" for literary resonance (Alice Through the Looking Glass = "Алиса в Зазеркалье").
- brainjacker → мозговой паразит — Ryan's derogatory term for Psyshock (body-jumping telepath). Not a single word in Russian; rendered as a vivid descriptive compound.
- waterthrower → водомёт — Len's pressurized water weapon. Standard Russian word for water cannon.
- Peepers → Соглядатай — Ryan's nickname for the Land's surveillance power. Rendered as a characterful Russian word for a spy/watcher.
