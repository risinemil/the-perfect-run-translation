# Chapter 16 (017: The Hidden Route) — New Terms

- **Narcinia** → Нарциния — Atom Cat's adopted sister, mentioned by him. Not in glossary.
- **Ischia** → Искья — Island where the Augusti's fortress-lab produces Bliss. Real Italian island.
- **Hector Manada** → Гектор Манада — CEO/founder of Dynamis. Mentioned by Shroud as someone who will eventually retire.
- **Invisiboy / Invisigirl** → Невидимчик / Невидимка — Ryan's improvised nicknames for Shroud before learning his name.
- **Plait-il** → Плэ-тиль — French expression ("I beg your pardon?"), used by Ryan. Kept as transliteration with italics.
- **DNA tracker** → ДНК-трекер — Tracking device that led enemies to Ryan's location.
- **Lucky Luke** → Лаки Люк — Franco-Belgian comic book character reference. Ryan compares himself to the fastest gunslinger.
- **ship graveyard** → кладбище кораблей — Location between Rust Town and Old Harbor where Shroud's base is hidden.
- **Orange Genome** → Оранжевый Геном — Genome color classification. Shroud's type (glass/light manipulation).
