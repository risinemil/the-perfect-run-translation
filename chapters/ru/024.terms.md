# New terms — Chapter 024 (25: Never Enuff Dakka)

- Mortimer → Мортимер — Killer Seven member. Scarecrow-themed sniper with skull mask. Refers to himself in third person. Transliterated.
- Sparrow → Воробей — Killer Seven member, original lineup. Shoots lasers; name unrelated to power. Translated (воробей = sparrow).
- the Vamp → Вамп — Killer Seven member (mentioned only). Not suited for direct combat. Transliterated.
- Night Terror → Ночной Ужас — Killer Seven member (mentioned only). Power works only in darkness. Translated.
- Fireman division / Firemen → Огнеборцы — Augusti soldiers using Firebrand knockoff Elixirs with pyrokinesis. Translated descriptively.
- Firebrand → Огнеклейм — Knockoff Elixir granting pyrokinesis. Transliterated with Russian phonetic adaptation.
- fugue state → состояние фуги — Genius-specific trance during invention. Established Genius-tech term.
- "Never Enuff Dakka" → «Дакки мало не бывает» — Chapter title. Warhammer 40k Ork reference ("dakka" = firepower). Adapted to Russian idiomatic pattern «мало не бывает».
- "Za Warudo" → «За Варудо» — JoJo's Bizarre Adventure reference (DIO's time-stop cry). Transliterated from Japanese via English.
- "Toki wo tomare" → «Токи во томарэ» — Japanese for "time, stop." JoJo reference. Transliterated phonetically.
- Stormtrooper → штурмовик — Star Wars reference (soldiers who can't aim). Standard Russian localization.
- Manada (as family/dynasty) → Манада — Dynamis ruling family surname. Used by Vulcan dismissively. Already in glossary as Enrique's surname.
